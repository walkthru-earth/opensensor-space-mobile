## Summary

The opensensor.space app copies readings from your own air quality sensors to your phone. It has
no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your location is never sent anywhere.

## What the app does with data

- **Sensor readings and files.** The app reads data files from sensors you connect over
  Bluetooth or your local Wi-Fi network and stores them on your phone. They leave your phone only
  when you choose to share or export them with your phone's share or save tools.
- **Sensor settings.** Wi-Fi names and passphrases you enter are sent only to your sensor over the
  encrypted Bluetooth link. The Wi-Fi sync token is stored encrypted on your phone. Neither is
  sent anywhere else.
- **Permissions.** Bluetooth (Nearby devices) to find and connect to your sensors, local network
  to reach them on your Wi-Fi, and notifications to show sync progress. Each is asked only when you
  use the feature. If you tap Near me on the public map, the app asks for your approximate
  location, uses it on your phone to move the map, and does not send or store it.

## Public sensor map (when available)

When the public map is on, the app downloads public map data anonymously. Like any internet
request this reaches the host with your IP address. We do not use it to identify you and our
hosting keeps no access logs. You can turn the public map off in Settings.

## Sharing a sensor publicly (optional, off by default)

You can choose to share one sensor's readings on the public map from that sensor's settings. Only
the readings, the sensor's public name and an area of about 5 km² are published. Its exact
location, identifiers, Wi-Fi details, PIN and token are never published. You can stop sharing
and delete that sensor's published data at any time from the same place.

## Data retention and deletion

Data on your phone stays until you forget a sensor, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
There is no account to delete. Published data of a shared sensor is deleted when you stop sharing
it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
